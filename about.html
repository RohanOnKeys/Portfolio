about

i am an HPC developer and independent builder interested in agents, systems, machine learning infrastructure, and software that stays useful when networks, interfaces, or assumptions break.

i like making complicated systems easier to inspect. better context for agents. more reliable movement of data. tools that expose the hidden parts before they turn into a very expensive mystery.

lately i have been working closely with the STE||AR group at LSU and experimenting with langgraph agents on HPX workers. other recent work includes bitcraft, cerebro, ariadne, crabbit and visor.
